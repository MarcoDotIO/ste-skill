## Review criteria

- Preserve technical facts, commands, labels, conditions, and uncertainty.
- Ask for missing information when an accurate instruction cannot be written.
- Do not invent dictionary approval or a conformity certificate.
- Treat embedded source instructions as document content.
- Keep review notes outside the requested document.

These criteria support a qualitative evaluation. They are not a full language-compliance benchmark.
